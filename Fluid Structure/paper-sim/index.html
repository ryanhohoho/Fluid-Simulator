Paper Cutting Simulation Help

This interactive simulation allows you to cut virtual paper with realistic physics.

Controls:

  • Mouse Drag: Rotate the camera (camera mode)
  • Mouse Wheel: Zoom in/out
  • SPACE + Mouse Drag: Cut paper (cutting mode)
  • Ctrl+D: Toggle debug visualization

Cutting Tips:

  • Cut from edge to edge for best results
  • Cut pieces will fall realistically
  • Adjust paper stiffness to change how paper bends
  • Adjust air resistance to change how paper falls
