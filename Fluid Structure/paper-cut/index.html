How to Use the Paper Cutting Simulation

Basic Controls

  • Space: Toggle between Camera Mode and Cutting Mode
  • Mouse Drag (Cutting Mode): Cut through the paper
  • Mouse Drag (Camera Mode): Rotate the camera
  • Scroll: Zoom in and out
  • Reset Button: Start over with a fresh sheet

Tips

  • For the most realistic cuts, move at a moderate speed
  • Try different paper textures for varied visual effects
  • Adjust air resistance to change how the cut pieces fall
  • Higher paper stiffness results in sharper, cleaner cuts
